Our Story

From Nonna's Kitchen to Our Brick Oven

Forno Rosso started in 1962 when Nonna Lucia began selling pizza from a small storefront, using a recipe carried over from her hometown outside Naples. Today, her grandchildren run the same oven, the same 48-hour fermented dough, and the same sauce recipe — nothing rushed, nothing shortcut.

Every pizza is hand-stretched to order and baked in under 90 seconds at 900°F, giving it that signature leopard-spotted crust and smoky char.
